Privacy Policy

PRimeLift

Last updated: July 2026
In short: PRimeLift collects no data for us. Your training data lives on your device and, if you want, in your private iCloud – not on our servers.

1. What we do not do

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising libraries and no third-party SDKs whatsoever. There are no accounts with us and no server for anything to be sent to.

2. Where your data lives

  • Plans, workouts, profile and custom exercises are stored locally on your device.
  • With iCloud sync enabled (the default) they are additionally stored in your private iCloud database. Only your Apple account has access – we do not.
  • Sync can be turned off in Settings. Your data then stays on the device; nothing is deleted.
  • Accent colour and catalogue language are kept in step across your devices via the iCloud key-value store.

3. Apple Health

  • On iPhone the app reads only when you explicitly tap the button in your profile: weight, height, body fat, lean mass, waist circumference, date of birth and sex.
  • On Apple Watch it records heart rate and active energy during a workout and saves the session to Health as strength training so it appears in your activity rings.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in the Health app. Training works without it.

4. AI coach

The AI coach is optional and not configured by default.

  • Your API key is stored exclusively in this device's keychain – not in iCloud, not with us.
  • Data leaves the device only when you request a suggestion, and then goes directly to the provider you chose. That provider's privacy policy applies.
  • The „share training data“ switch controls whether body metrics and history are included. With it off, they stay on the device.
  • Stated limitations (an injured shoulder, say) are always included. A plan that loads an injury would not be data-thrifty, it would be dangerous.
  • If you use Apple Intelligence, processing stays on the device.

5. Export

The history export produces text that you copy or share yourself. Nothing is transmitted automatically.

6. Your rights

  • You can delete plans, workouts and custom exercises inside the app.
  • Data stored in iCloud is managed through your Apple account.
  • Uninstalling removes the local data.

7. Changes

This policy is updated when data processing changes. Material changes are communicated through app updates.

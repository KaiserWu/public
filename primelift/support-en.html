Questions, bugs or requests? Write to me – I read every message myself.

1. Frequently asked questions

  • My plans do not show up on the watch. The watch gets its data through iCloud, not through a direct link to the iPhone. That takes a moment and requires both devices to be signed in to the same Apple account with iCloud sync enabled in Settings.
  • The rest timer on the watch stops. Grant PRimeLift access to Health. Without a running workout session, watchOS suspends the app as soon as you lower your wrist.
  • The AI coach does not respond. Check Settings → AI coach for provider, model and key. The key is stored on this device only – on a new device it has to be entered again.
  • Exercise names are in the wrong language. Settings → Exercises → Catalogue language. The choice applies to the watch as well.
  • I want to delete my data. Plans, workouts and custom exercises can be removed inside the app. Anything in iCloud is managed through your Apple account.

2. Reporting a problem

Please send an email with your device, iOS version and – if possible – the steps that lead to the problem. Screenshots help a lot.
